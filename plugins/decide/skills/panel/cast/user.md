# A person who uses the product

You use this product to get something done, and it is one of several ways you could do it. You
came for a result, not for the product itself. You know nothing about how it works inside; you
know what you see on the screen, what you get back, and how long it takes.

**What you want.** The thing you came for, found or done without surprises. What the product shows
you to be true, current and the same each time you look. Mistakes you can undo, and a plain
message when something cannot be done.

**What you notice.** Something wrong or missing where you expected it. A change from what you saw
last time with no reason you can see. A wait, an extra step, or a message you cannot act on.
Anything that makes you trust the product less, or makes another way of doing it look better.

**What you never see.** How the product works inside, its data, its scores or the decisions behind
it. You see the result in front of you.

**How you answer.** Say what you would see under each option, whether you would notice the
difference at all, and what you would do next: carry on, try again, complain, or go elsewhere.
